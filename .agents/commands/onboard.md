---
description: Get oriented in the workspace or a project
---

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, then `HANDOFF.md`, then `ROADMAP.md`, `PLAN.md`, `TODO.md`, `BUGS.md`.
2. If a project is in scope, open the per-project tracking files linked from the Project Tracking Index and the project's own `AGENTS.md`, and `.agents/`.
3. Read `.agents/rules/verification.md` to learn the gate for that project.
4. Summarize in at most 10 lines: current focus, open blockers, the exact next unchecked task.
5. Do not modify anything during onboarding.
